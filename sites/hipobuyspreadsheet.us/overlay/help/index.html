What HipoBuy is

Does HipoBuy sell the items?
No. It is a purchasing agent: it buys from third-party Chinese shops in your name, stores the goods, then books an international line after you confirm warehouse photos.
Language of the official site
The official language selector includes several European languages plus English and Chinese. Our 29 Sep 2026 lab used the public estimator API. Address format still has to match the destination country.
Why is the on-site catalogue empty?
English (sneakers, hoodie). /api/products/ on this host 404ed until 30 Sep 2026 nginx rewrite.

What you pay

How many payments?
Two: goods plus China domestic first, international freight after QC photos. You can consolidate in warehouse.
Why USD?
USD is the platform default. Switching the selector to EUR/GBP has been observed to change the symbol without converting the number. Treat figures as dollars. Open hipobuy.com/estimation before you book.
Volumetric weight?
Usually L×W×H/8000. Billed = max(actual, volumetric). Lab carton 29 Sep 2026: 1000 g, 35×25×10 cm, clothes/common goods.

CBP and duties — educational

Who pays duties?
US treatment follows the booked SKU. Read CBP duty basics. This desk does not invent a de-minimis dollar figure for your carton. No under-declaration tips.

Warehouse

Can I combine orders?
Yes. Storage limits are in official help and can change.
QC photos?
Yes, plus optional extra photos. Disputes are easier while goods remain in the warehouse.
What cannot ship?
Tobacco, alcohol, medicines, banned goods. Liquids/powders/pastes may need extra paperwork.

If something is wrong

Lighter than estimated?
Official FAQ covers estimated vs actual billed weight and excess postage refunds.
Where do I claim?
Only on hipobuy.com. Official help · Discord · Reddit.
Invite in the title?
No. VGEICZNX0 lives on the coupon URL and is re-typed in-app.
How do I pay?
The official site lists PayPal, Visa, Mastercard and wallet top-up. Balance refunds are in their FAQ, not on this desk.
Why restricted or price zero?
The source link is not buyable through the agent, or the price could not be read. Do not count on a card without a real price and variants. HipoBuy also says it cannot buy cross-border marketplace listings.

Estimator and tickets

Open hipobuy.com/estimation with destination United States after QC photos. Write United States in tickets, not EU. FedEx-tax-prepaid SKUs billed volumetric 2000 g on the same 1000 g lab carton — read last-mile, not only the dollar. Two cartons after a QC split are two estimates. The 700 g USA widget leftover on some templates is not this lab. Insurance is chosen per line in HipoBuy. Finds stay on hipobuyspreadsheet.net.
