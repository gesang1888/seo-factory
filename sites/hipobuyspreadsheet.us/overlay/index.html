Customs — educational

US treatment follows the booked product. CBP duty basics. This desk does not invent a de-minimis dollar figure. No under-declaration tips.

Tobacco, alcohol, medicines, banned goods do not travel. Restricted or zero-price cards are not buyable. If billed weight is lighter than estimated, official FAQ covers excess postage — not this HTML file. Discord and Reddit belong to the platform.
