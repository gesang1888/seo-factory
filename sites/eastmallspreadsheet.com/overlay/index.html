The 90-day calendar

Treat the EastMallBuy warehouse like a calendar: inbound QC this week, inbound next week, one outbound when the pallet is boringly complete. Photograph the storage-fee schedule in the app; promotions that say 90 days free still have overtime rules.

Photos while you wait

Free or cheap QC is useful only if you wait for it. Returning a size before consolidation is cheaper than returning a finished parcel. EastMallBuy photos should be checked against the seller's measurement table, not against Instagram.

Do not pick EastMallBuy if

If you need same-day outbound or you refuse to hold goods, EastMallBuy's storage story is wasted. Use an agent that prices speed, not dwell time.
