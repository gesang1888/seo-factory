The fifteen questions that come back most

Where is CBP detail?

Kept on /de-minimis-us-litbuy/ and /cbp-declared-value-us/. This homepage does not invent a dollar de-minimis.

USPS times?

Kept on /litbuy-shipping-times-us/.

Does LitBuy sell the goods?

No. It is a purchasing agent: two payments, warehouse photos, then an international SKU.

Where is the catalogue?

On this homepage from this host’s /api/products/. Thousands of finds stay on w2clinks.

Invite in the title?

No. The affiliate URL is the Register button only.

Is destination the United States a TLD?

The estimator wants a country code. This hostname is a desk, not a customs territory.

Free storage?

Official Help Center: 90 free days from stocked/listed, 120-day max.

Volumetric weight?

Most air SKUs: L×W×H/8000 in kilograms. The widget is geometry, not a quote.

Customs?

Follows the booked SKU. No under-declaration tips. Use the official destination source the morning you ship.

Coupons in this title?

No. Live codes sit on the coupon URL, not in this title.

QC photos?

In-app after warehouse arrival.

USD digits?

The official currency switcher has been observed to change the symbol without converting the number.

301 to another LitBuy TLD?

No. Same-agent country hosts stay independent.

Is this litbuy.com?

No. Independent desk.

Local search words?

The index is English. Local words often return zero cards.

Where to ask when this is not enough

Official in-app chat. This desk does not operate your LitBuy account. Ranked coupon and customs URLs stay on this host.
