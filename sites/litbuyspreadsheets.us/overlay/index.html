Questions

Does LitBuy sell the goods on this site?

No. LitBuy buys from Chinese shops in your name. Checkout stays on litbuy.com.

Can I pay here?

No. Pay on litbuy.com. This page shows USD as display. Live freight is https://litbuy.com/shipping-estimate with destination United States.

Which estimator country?

United States — not this TLD, not Canada. US ZIP (form 10001).

Does QC happen at USPS?

No. Photos are taken in the China warehouse. USPS or FedEx is last-mile after you accept QC.

Who pays duty?

The booked SKU. This page does not invent a de-minimis dollar. Source: https://www.cbp.gov/ (CBP).

Is a Canada Post SKU the same?

No. A Canadian postal code is a different country.

Why do local words return zero cards?

The finds index is English (sneakers, hoodie, jacket).

Where is the invite code?

Not in this title. Existing coupon URLs on litbuyspreadsheets.us stay.
