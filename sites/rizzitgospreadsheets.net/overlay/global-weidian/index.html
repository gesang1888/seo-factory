What you are looking at

global.weidian.com is Weidian’s overseas catalog, launched as a product grid. Selecting a product opens the normal Weidian item, carrying a CPS offer id. Overseas visitors can get a panel titled around shopping platforms: Traceable, Buy Now, Copy Link. The 18 November view listed USFans, OOPBUY, RizzitGO, then FishGoo.

Copy Link is how those rows spread into chats and spreadsheets. Buy Now should open RizzitGO on that product. If it opens only the RizzitGO homepage, paste the Weidian item link into RizzitGO search yourself.

Order the item on RizzitGO

  1. Copy the Weidian item URL that contains itemID.
  2. Open RizzitGO with rno=D5AFC9 and paste the URL into search.
  3. Check the parsed title, price, size and domestic freight before you pay.
  4. Use warehouse photos on RizzitGO before you pay international shipping. This spreadsheet’s QC notes are a preview, not the photo of your unit.

Keep spreadsheet buy buttons on RizzitGO. A link back to weidian.com/item.html?offerId= puts the shared list in front of the buyer again.

Traceable is a list mark

It means Weidian is offering RizzitGO as a way to purchase that item from abroad. It is not a customs clearance promise and it is not a coupon by itself. Shipping lines still come from RizzitGO at checkout. The shipping guide on this desk explains how to read them; the live price is in the RizzitGO estimator.
