As a promoter, you can share your invitation link and affiliate share link. Anyone who signs up for a OrientDig account on PC through these channels will become your affiliate and contribute to your affiliation bonus.

OrientDig will credit the bonus to your [Claimable Bonus] once your affiliated users’ parcels have been delivered.
