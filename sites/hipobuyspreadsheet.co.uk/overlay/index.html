Questions

Does HipoBuy sell the goods on this site?

No. HipoBuy is a purchasing agent. hipobuyspreadsheet.co.uk is a spreadsheet for a UK door. Orders, payment and claims stay on hipobuy.com.

Can I pay on this hostname?

No. Register and pay only on hipobuy.com. Display currency here is GBP; the live amount is in the app.

Which estimator country do I pick?

United Kingdom — not this TLD, not EU, not Ireland. Open https://hipobuy.com/estimation before you pay freight. UK postcode (form SW1A 1AA).

Does QC happen in the UK?

No. Warehouse photos are taken in China. Royal Mail is last-mile after you accept QC.

Who pays import VAT?

The booked SKU decides prepaid vs collect. Read https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad (HMRC) the morning you ship. This page does not invent a declared value.

Is Northern Ireland the same SKU?

Often not. Northern Ireland can be another carrier product than mainland Royal Mail. Pick the live line in the app.

Why do local words return zero cards?

The finds index is English (sneakers, hoodie, jacket). That is not an empty shop.

Where is the invite code?

Not in this title. Coupon URLs already on hipobuyspreadsheet.co.uk stay as they are.
