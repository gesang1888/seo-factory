Official new-user welcome pack: 14 shipping coupons, up to ¥800

Eligible new accounts receive the pack after registration. The official breakdown is:

  • ¥20 off international shipping, no minimum — 5 coupons
  • ¥50 off shipping over ¥300 — 5 coupons
  • ¥90 off shipping over ¥500 — 2 coupons
  • ¥120 off shipping over ¥600 — 1 coupon
  • ¥150 off shipping over ¥700 — 1 coupon

Only one coupon can be used per parcel. The coupons apply to international freight, not product payments or value-added services, and expire 90 days after issue. Official welcome-pack terms.

2026SG50: official blog lists a $7 coupon

Sign in, go to My Coupons / Coupon Center, enter 2026SG50, then select the coupon at checkout. Sugargoo controls eligibility and expiry, so verify the coupon in your own account before ordering. Official redemption guide.

Where to apply Sugargoo coupons

  1. Register or sign in on Sugargoo.
  2. Open My Account → Coupons (or Packing Center → My Coupons).
  3. Check the coupon type, minimum spend and expiry.
  4. At parcel checkout, select one eligible shipping coupon and confirm the final fee.
