A purchasing agent is an intermediary, not a shop

SugarGoo does not sell the goods. It buys for you in Chinese shops that do not ship abroad, photographs in the warehouse, stores, then you book the international line. This hostname is not a destination — pick a country in the official estimator.

That changes the path: you pay twice (goods plus China domestic, later international freight), you wait twice, and you can consolidate or switch SKU in between. sugargoospreadsheetnow.com is an independent information desk, not sugargoo.com.

Does SugarGoo sell the goods on this site?

How many times do I pay?

Twice: first the goods plus China domestic shipping into the warehouse, later the international line to a country in the estimator. Between those payments you can consolidate. This desk never charges you; SugarGoo does, in-app. Warehouse photos sit between the two payments. Do not book international freight to a country in the estimator until you have accepted QC in-app.

Why does the official site show dollars while this desk shows USD?

On the official SPA the currency switcher has been observed to change the symbol without converting the digits. This desk converts the China warehouse card to USD as a dated display (X-Rates 30 Sep 2026), not a checkout price. Re-read the number in-app the morning you pay. What is volumetric weight?

Most air SKUs bill max(actual, L×W×H/divisor) with the result in kilograms (the widget shows grams). A puffy jacket is light on the scale and expensive in volume. Geometry only — live money is in https://www.sugargoo.com/freight-estimate with destination a country in the estimator. The widget uses 35×25×10 cm and 1000 g as a lab habit; your carton will differ. Open https://www.sugargoo.com/freight-estimate with destination a country in the estimator before you pay freight.

Who pays duties into a country in the estimator?

It is a property of the booked SKU (tax-free, prepaid, or collect). Collect can add a last-mile handling fee the estimator often hides. Educational source for this desk: the destination customs site named in the estimator. No under-declaration tips. Prepaid vs collect is chosen when you submit the parcel, not when you buy the goods. Read the SKU heading and the destination customs site named in the estimator the same morning.

Is there a tax threshold I should game?

No. Thresholds, IOSS and de-minimis change and are not a reason to invent a declared value. Read the destination customs site named in the estimator and the SKU terms the morning you ship. This desk does not coach a number. A screenshot of a threshold from Discord is not a filing. the destination customs site named in the estimator is the source this desk names. We will not invent a declared value for a country in the estimator.

Can I consolidate several orders?

Do they photograph the goods?

Yes, warehouse QC photos land in the app after the piece arrives in China. Extra angles are often a paid add-on. What cannot ship?

Tobacco, alcohol, medicines and banned goods do not travel. Liquids, powders and pastes can need dangerous-goods paperwork. That is platform rule, not the destination customs site named in the estimator law. Restricted / price-0 cards on this catalogue are not buyable.
