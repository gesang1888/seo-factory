SugarGoo FAQ for Australia buyers on sugargoospreadsheet.au.

Does SugarGoo sell the goods on this site?

No. SugarGoo is a purchasing agent. sugargoospreadsheet.au is a spreadsheet for an Australian door. Orders, payment and claims stay on www.sugargoo.com.

Can I pay on this hostname?

No. Register and pay only on www.sugargoo.com. Display currency here is AUD; the live amount is in the app.

Which estimator country do I pick?

Australia — not this TLD, not EU, not New Zealand. Open https://www.sugargoo.com/freight-estimate before you pay freight. Australian postcode (form 2000).

Does QC happen in Australia?

No. Warehouse photos are taken in China. Australia Post is last-mile after you accept QC.

Who pays import VAT?

The booked SKU decides prepaid vs collect. Read https://www.abf.gov.au/ (ABF) the morning you ship. This page does not invent a declared value.

Is New Zealand the same SKU?

Often not. New Zealand can be another carrier product than mainland Australia Post. Pick the live line in the app.

Why do local words return zero cards?

The finds index is English (sneakers, hoodie, jacket). That is not an empty shop.

Where is the invite code?

Not in this title. Coupon URLs already on sugargoospreadsheet.au stay as they are.
