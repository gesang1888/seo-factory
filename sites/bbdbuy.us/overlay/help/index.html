Does BBDBuy sell the goods on this site?

No. BBDBuy is a purchasing agent: it buys from Chinese third-party shops in your name, photographs the parcel in its warehouse, then you book an international SKU. bbdbuy.us is an independent information desk. Orders, payment and claims stay on https://www.bbdbuy.com/. The cards on bbdbuy.us are a reading of the finds index, not stock BBDBuy owns. Payment never happens on this hostname. How many times do I pay?

Twice: first the goods plus China domestic shipping into the warehouse, later the international line to the United States. Between those payments you can consolidate. This desk never charges you; BBDBuy does, in-app. Warehouse photos sit between the two payments. Do not book international freight to the United States until you have accepted QC in-app.

What cannot ship?

Tobacco, alcohol, medicines and banned goods do not travel. Liquids, powders and pastes can need dangerous-goods paperwork. That is platform rule, not CBP law. Restricted / price-0 cards on this catalogue are not buyable. Restricted is a platform purchase block, not a CBP seizure notice. Price-0 cards on bbdbuy.us are not a shop error you can checkout.
