Questions

Does LoveGoBuy sell the goods on this site?

No. LoveGoBuy is the purchasing agent. Pay on www.lovegobuy.com.

Can I pay here?

No. Pay on www.lovegobuy.com. Display here is CAD. Live freight is https://www.lovegobuy.com/shipping/estimation/index with destination Canada.

Which estimator country?

Canada — not this TLD, not US. Canadian postal code (A1A 1A1).

Does QC happen at Canada Post?

No. Photos are taken in China. Canada Post is last-mile after you accept QC.

Who pays GST/HST on import?

The booked SKU. Read https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/ (CBSA) the morning you ship. This page does not invent a declared value.

Is a USPS SKU the same?

No. A US ZIP is a different country.

Why do French catalogue words return zero?

The index keys are English (sneakers, hoodie, jacket).

Where is the invite code?

Not in this title. Ranked coupon URLs on lovegobuyspreadsheet.ca stay as they are.
