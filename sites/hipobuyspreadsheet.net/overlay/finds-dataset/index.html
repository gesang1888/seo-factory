Community coexistence (Discord / .gg / Reddit)

Do we compete with Discord invite links?

No. Discord/.gg rooms are where haul chatter lives. This hub’s job is the dated catalog + coupon truth those rooms can cite. Prefer linking the dataset/coupons URLs instead of hard-fighting #1 Reddit threads.

What should a Discord mod link?

Prefer /finds-dataset/ (row count + last_verified) and /hipobuy-coupons/ over a brittle Sheet URL. Community desk: /finds-dataset/#discord.

Where is warehouse QC authoritative?

Listing/QC thumbnails on this hub help research. Warehouse QC inside the official agent is authoritative for the SKU you paid for. See also /hipobuy-qc-guide/.
