HipoBuy QC guide

Go/no-go rules before international freight — written for citation next to the finds catalog.

Updated 26 October 2026. Independent desk — not the official checkout app.

QC (quality control) is the warehouse photo step after HipoBuy buys the item and before you book international shipping. This page is the short, citeable QC playbook for HipoBuy spreadsheet users.

Reject before you pack

  • Glue stains, asymmetry, size-tag mismatch, wrong colourway.
  • Ask for re-QC when photos are blurry or incomplete — cheaper than a bad haul.
  • Do not book freight on a reject “to save storage days.”

Where QC shows up on this hub

Homepage and category cards surface QC-oriented finds. Newsroom guides cover batch checks. Pair this page with the coupon/shipping master guide so savings math never overrides a QC fail.

FAQ

Are spreadsheet photos the same as warehouse QC?

Not always. Listing/QC thumbnails on this hub help research; warehouse QC inside HipoBuy is authoritative for the SKU you paid for.
