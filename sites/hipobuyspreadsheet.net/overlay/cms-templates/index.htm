What is Hipobuy?

Hipobuy.com is a Chinese shopping agent (taobao agent / freight forwarder) that helps international buyers purchase products from Weidian, Taobao, 1688 and other Chinese marketplaces. It's especially popular for clothing, sneakers and streetwear, offering competitive USD pricing, QC (Quality Control) photos and consolidated international shipping.

Hipobuy is one of several Chinese shopping agents used to purchase from domestic marketplaces. This site is an independent browsing guide and does not represent Hipobuy, sellers or marketplaces.

How do I buy from Hipobuy?

If you're new to Hipobuy, here's the short version of the workflow our community uses. For the full step-by-step, see our News & Guides.

1. Find the items

Browse this Hipobuy spreadsheet by category or use the search box. Open the source listing and confirm that its photos, variants, price and availability still match.

2. Use product links

Copy the product link into Hipobuy (or your preferred agent: CNFans, Kakobuy, Mulebuy, Sugargoo, AllChinaBuy, Loongbuy). The agent fetches the listing and lets you choose size, color, version and batch.

3. View QC photos & ship

Once the item arrives at the warehouse you'll receive QC photos. Approve them, pick a shipping line (faster lines cost more), and your parcel ships internationally with tracking.
