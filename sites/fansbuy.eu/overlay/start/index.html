A purchasing agent is a middleman, not a shop

FansBuy does not sell its own goods. It buys for you in Chinese shops that do not ship abroad, receives the parcel in the warehouse, photographs it, stores it, and ships to a member state when you decide.

That changes everything: you pay twice (first the product, later international), you wait twice, and in between you can still cancel, consolidate or switch lines. Payment and tickets stay on https://fansbuy.com/.

Step-by-step guide

Paste a Chinese link, or search in the app

FansBuy starts the same way: paste a Taobao, 1688 or Weidian link, or type the name. The Chinese shop sees FansBuy; you later see warehouse photos and a line to a member state.

If the search bar does not read the link, the next step is a manual form: name, size, colour and price in yuan. International freight is paid later, from the warehouse.

A name that misleads

A spreadsheet is not an Excel file

In English the word still sounds like a grid of rows and columns. Here «spreadsheet» means a catalogue of product cards with a photo, a brand, a reference price and the link you paste into the agent.

What you see on w2clinks are cards, not cells. Each fiche has the link FansBuy needs.

Nine statuses, three screens

You first pay the product plus domestic China freight to the warehouse. International comes later, when you pick a line to a member state. “Why is it stuck?” is almost always: you are looking at the wrong screen.

  • Order SubmittedOrder sent, product paid in China.
  • Order PlacedFansBuy buys in the Chinese shop in your name.
  • Seller ShippedThe Chinese seller has shipped.
  • Arrived at WarehouseArrived at the warehouse.
  • Inspection & StorageCheck, photos and storage. Live labels sit in the app.
  • Shipping RequestedYou consolidate and book the international line.
  • Parcel PackedThe box is packed.
  • ShippedDeparture from China.
  • DeliveredDelivered; confirm receipt in the app.

The first stretch lives under orders, then warehouse, then the parcel you submit. The weight you pay is almost never the scale alone

Many lines bill the greater of scale and volume. A common divisor is L×W×H (cm) / 8000. A down jacket is light and bulky: volume decides.

Example: 40×40×3 cm is 4800 cm³, divided by 8000 is 600 g volume at 200 g real weight. You enter your measurements in the public estimator — no login — destination not a customs territory.

Many products you cannot buy even if they appear

On the official site you will see cards without a price, or a manual form instead of a shop card. That is not a bug of this homepage: the source link is not buyable through the agent, or the price could not be read.

Rule: without a real price and variants, do not order. Tobacco, alcohol and medicines do not travel. Restricted is a purchase block, not a taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu seizure notice. FansBuy does not sell its own stock.
