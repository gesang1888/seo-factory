Questions

Does OOTDBuy sell the goods on this site?

No. OOTDBuy is a purchasing agent: it buys from Chinese third-party shops in your name, photographs the parcel in its warehouse, then you book an international SKU. ootdbuyspreadsheet.co.uk is a spreadsheet for a UK door. Orders, payment and claims stay on ootdbuy.com.

Can I pay on this hostname?

No. Register and pay only on ootdbuy.com. ootdbuyspreadsheet.co.uk never takes a card. Display currency here is GBP; the live amount is in the app.

Which estimator country do I pick?

United Kingdom — not EU, not Ireland. Open https://ootdbuy.com/estimation/ before you pay freight. UK postcode.

Does QC happen in the UK?

No. Warehouse photos are taken in China. Royal Mail is last-mile after you accept QC. Extra angles are an in-app add-on.

Who pays import VAT?

The booked SKU decides prepaid vs collect. Royal Mail can add a handling fee. Read https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad (HMRC) the morning you ship. This page does not invent a declared value.

Is Northern Ireland the same SKU?

Often not. Northern Ireland can be another carrier product than mainland Royal Mail. Pick the live line in the app; do not swap the country name on this desk.

Why do Dutch words return zero cards?

They should — this desk is English for UK buyers. Local-language catalogue words often return zero because the index keys are sneakers, hoodie, jacket.

Where is the invite code?

Not in this title. Coupon and invite URLs already on ootdbuyspreadsheet.co.uk stay as they are. New cluster titles do not paste a code.
