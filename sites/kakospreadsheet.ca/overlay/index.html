Does Kakobuy sell the goods on this site?

No. Kakobuy is a purchasing agent: it buys from Chinese third-party shops in your name, photographs the parcel in its warehouse, then you book an international SKU. kakospreadsheet.ca is an independent information desk. How many times do I pay?

Twice: first the goods plus China domestic shipping into the warehouse, later the international line to Canada. Between those payments you can consolidate. This desk never charges you; Kakobuy does, in-app. Warehouse photos sit between the two payments. Do not book international freight to Canada until you have accepted QC in-app.

What is volumetric weight?

Most air SKUs bill max(actual, L×W×H/divisor) with the result in kilograms (the widget shows grams). A puffy jacket is light on the scale and expensive in volume. Who pays duties into Canada?

Who pays duties into Canada is on the booked SKU. A broker or last-mile can add a collect fee. Educational source: CBSA. Use a Canadian postal code in the estimator, not a US ZIP. Do they photograph the goods?

Yes, warehouse QC photos land in the app after the piece arrives in China. Extra angles are often a paid add-on. What cannot ship?

Tobacco, alcohol, medicines and banned goods do not travel. Liquids, powders and pastes can need dangerous-goods paperwork. That is platform rule, not CBSA law. Restricted / price-0 cards on this catalogue are not buyable. Restricted is a platform purchase block, not a CBSA seizure notice. Price-0 cards on kakospreadsheet.ca are not a shop error you can checkout.

Why is a card restricted or priced 0?

The source link is not available for agent purchase, or the price could not be read. Do not plan a haul around a zero. Transborder shops are often out of scope. Check the live Kakobuy product page. Price 0 often means the Weidian or Taobao link died. Open the live Kakobuy product page the same day; do not plan the haul on this HTML.
