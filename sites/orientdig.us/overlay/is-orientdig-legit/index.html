Is OrientDig legal in the United States?

Using a purchasing agent is not itself a crime. Restricted goods, platform rules and the booked SKU still apply. Read https://www.cbp.gov/.

Legal here means the agent model. It does not mean every Taobao SKU is allowed through CBP.

Is OrientDig safe to use?

Safe means warehouse QC before freight, payment in the official app, and a real US ZIP — not a promise the parcel skips CBP.
