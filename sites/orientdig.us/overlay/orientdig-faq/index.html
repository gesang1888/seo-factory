OrientDig FAQ for US Buyers (2026)

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ for US buyers on orientdig.us: index, QC, USPS, CBP. Not checkout.

Does this site sell the goods?

No. Orders stay on orientdig.com. orientdig.us lists finds for US buyers.

Cards are an index, not stock.

What is the OrientDig spreadsheet?

A shortlist of warehouse-checked SKUs for a US ZIP. Not Excel.

Keywords: OrientDig spreadsheet, orientdig spreadsheet usa, orientdig sheet.

How do I use QC photos?

They land in-app while the piece is in China.

USPS will not retake a warehouse photo.

How does shipping to the USA work?

Estimator destination US. Last-mile often USPS. Live quote: https://orientdig.com/estimation/.

US ZIP. No invented transit days.

Who pays import charges?

The booked SKU. Collect can add a fee. Source: https://www.cbp.gov/. This desk does not invent a de-minimis dollar.

No declared-value coaching.

Why USD here?

Display conversion of the China card. Checkout is whatever orientdig.com shows that morning.

Can I use a Canadian postal code?

No. That is the wrong country for this site.

Where do I register?

Only on orientdig.com. orientdig.us does not take cards.

Can I use a Canadian postal code?

No. Use a US ZIP — US ZIP. Destination United States in https://orientdig.com/estimation/.

Where do I pay?

On orientdig.com. Last mile is often USPS.

When do I book freight?

After QC photos. Storage days are in Help on orientdig.com.

Who pays duty?

The booked SKU. Read https://www.cbp.gov/ (CBP). This page does not invent a de-minimis dollar.
