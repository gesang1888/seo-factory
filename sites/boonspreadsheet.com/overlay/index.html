Warehouse

Park goods in BoonBuy's warehouse until the haul is dense enough that one outbound parcel beats three thin ones. Do not assume unlimited free days — check the current storage clock in the BoonBuy app before you let a haul sit through a sales week.

QC

BoonBuy QC photos are a go/no-go gate, not a fashion lookbook. Reject on glue stains, asymmetry, and size-tag mismatch before you submit the parcel. Re-QC costs time; shipping a reject costs more.

Who should skip it

Skip BoonBuy if you need same-week branded triangular shipping paperwork or if you only buy from one domestic boutique. It is a China marketplace agent (Weidian / Taobao / 1688), not a US return desk.

Questions this hub actually answers

Does the 30% shipping offer apply to every line?

No. Read the BoonBuy coupon drawer for the line names it actually attaches to. Volumetric lines and private lines are often excluded.

When should I ship vs wait?

Ship when added storage fees would exceed the extra freight of a second parcel. That crossover is haul-specific; this page does not invent a universal day count.
