Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying from China in 2026

What is HubBuy.net and how is it different from HubBuy.com?

HubBuy.net is this free strategy and guide website — it's where you come to learn how to shop from China, compare products, and find the best deals. HubBuy.com is the actual purchasing agent platform where you place real orders. Think of .net as the map and .com as the store.

What is a purchasing agent (daigou) and why do I need one?

Chinese platforms like Taobao and Weidian are mostly designed for domestic Chinese customers and don't ship internationally. A purchasing agent (daigou) like HubBuy.com acts as your middleman: they buy the item on your behalf, receive it at their Chinese warehouse, perform quality checks, and then ship it to your international address. They also handle any language barriers and payment issues.

How do I place an order on HubBuy.com?

It's straightforward. Find a product you want using our guides on this site, copy the Taobao/Weidian product link, and paste it into the order form on HubBuy.com. You'll see the estimated price in your currency, choose your size/variant, and pay. HubBuy handles everything from there. Don't forget to use coupon code SHIP for shipping savings.

How long does shipping from China take?

Shipping times vary by route and service level. Budget lines typically take 15–30 days; standard lines 10–18 days; and express lines 5–10 days. HubBuy offers multiple shipping options so you can choose based on your budget and timeline. All shipments include tracking numbers and are covered by insurance on request.

Are the products on the guides authentic?

Our guides cover a wide range of goods sold on Chinese platforms — including factory-direct products, OEM goods, and official brand items sold domestically in China. We always note what type of product each guide covers. If you have doubts about a specific item, HubBuy.com's QC photos give you a clear view of the actual product before it ships.

Can I get help if I can't find what I'm looking for?

Absolutely. Join our community on Discord or Telegram. Drop a product image or description and our community will help you find the right link. You can also contact the HubBuy.com support team directly for agent-specific questions.
