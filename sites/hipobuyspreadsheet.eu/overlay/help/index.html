What HipoBuy is, and which language it uses

Does HipoBuy sell the goods on this site?

No. HipoBuy is a purchasing agent: it buys from Chinese third-party shops in your name, photographs the parcel in its warehouse, then you book an international SKU. hipobuyspreadsheet.eu is an independent information desk. Orders, payment and claims stay on https://hipobuy.com/. The cards on hipobuyspreadsheet.eu are a reading of the finds index, not stock HipoBuy owns. Payment never happens on this hostname. The delivery address uses a a real member-state address, not this hostname.

The official app language switcher is independent of this desk. We measured the public site on 6 Oct 2026. Field names in the estimator often stay English even when the chrome is translated. The delivery address must still match a member state. Switching chrome language on hipobuyspreadsheet.eu does not change the official app. Confirm the in-app language the morning you order toward a member state.

What you will pay

How many times do I pay?

Twice: first the goods plus China domestic shipping into the warehouse, later the international line to a member state. Between those payments you can consolidate. This desk never charges you; HipoBuy does, in-app. Warehouse photos sit between the two payments. Do not book international freight to a member state until you have accepted QC in-app.

Why does the official site show dollars while this desk shows EUR?

On the official SPA the currency switcher has been observed to change the symbol without converting the digits. This desk converts the China warehouse card to EUR as a dated display (X-Rates 6 Oct 2026), not a checkout price. Re-read the number in-app the morning you pay. If the official SPA still prints USD digits after you pick EUR, trust the digits and https://hipobuy.com/estimation, not this HTML. Display rate dated 6 Oct 2026.

How do I pay?

Only inside https://hipobuy.com/. Typical rails are card, PayPal and balance top-up — confirm in the official FAQ the same day. hipobuyspreadsheet.eu never takes card data and does not store a saved method. Register links on this desk never collect card numbers. Those fields exist only on https://hipobuy.com/. hipobuyspreadsheet.eu has no saved method.

What is volumetric weight?

Most air SKUs bill max(actual, L×W×H/divisor) with the result in kilograms (the widget shows grams). A puffy jacket is light on the scale and expensive in volume. Geometry only — live money is in https://hipobuy.com/estimation with destination a member state. The widget uses 35×25×10 cm and 1000 g as a lab habit; your carton will differ. Open https://hipobuy.com/estimation with destination a member state before you pay freight.

Import into a member state

Who pays duties into a member state?

This hostname is not a customs territory. Duties follow the country you pick in the official estimator, not the TLD. Read that country’s customs site the morning you ship. No under-declaration. Prepaid vs collect is chosen when you submit the parcel, not when you buy the goods. Read the SKU heading and taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu the same morning.

Is there a tax threshold I should game?

A hub TLD has no de-minimis of its own. Pick a real country in the estimator. This desk does not invent a threshold for “EU” as one destination. A screenshot of a threshold from Discord is not a filing. taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu is the source this desk names. We will not invent a declared value for a member state.

Do they photograph the goods?

Yes, warehouse QC photos land in the app after the piece arrives in China. Extra angles are often a paid add-on. Disputes are easier while it is still in the warehouse, not after it has cleared last-mile. If a photo is blurry, buy another angle in-app before the piece leaves China toward a member state. This desk cannot open a ticket.

What cannot ship?

Tobacco, alcohol, medicines and banned goods do not travel. Liquids, powders and pastes can need dangerous-goods paperwork. That is platform rule, not taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu law. Restricted / price-0 cards on this catalogue are not buyable. Restricted is a platform purchase block, not a taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu seizure notice. Price-0 cards on hipobuyspreadsheet.eu are not a shop error you can checkout.

Why is a card restricted or priced 0?

The source link is not available for agent purchase, or the price could not be read. Do not plan a haul around a zero. Transborder shops are often out of scope. Check the live HipoBuy product page. Price 0 often means the Weidian or Taobao link died. Open the live HipoBuy product page the same day; do not plan the haul on this HTML.
