Guide

First HipoBuy order, from a member state

Copy the shop link, paste it into the agent, check the warehouse photo, consolidate, then pick destination not a customs territory — pick ES/IE/IT. The delivery address uses a a real member-state address, not this hostname.

1. Open a card on w2clinks

One of the thirty-three categories, a photo and the shop link. That is the catalogue, not an Excel file.

2. Paste it on the official site

Payment and tickets stay on https://hipobuy.com/. This desk cannot see your account. The public paste bar is «Goods/shop's link or name». Official Help Center is a SPA (https://hipobuy.com/help). Desks document 90 free warehouse days from Stored; confirm that live Help copy the morning you ship (6 Oct 2026). This dest does not invent a storage window as a coupon. Orders stay on hipobuy.com.

3. Photo, then consolidate, then ship

On 6 Oct 2026 freight figures come only from the official estimator: pick the country, not this hostname. This desk does not invent a line.

Nine statuses, three screens

First the product plus domestic freight to the warehouse. International later. “Why is it stuck?” is almost always the wrong screen — orders, then warehouse, then the parcel.

  • Order SubmittedOrder sent, product paid in China.
  • Order PlacedHipoBuy buys in the Chinese shop in your name.
  • Seller ShippedThe Chinese seller has shipped.
  • Arrived at WarehouseArrived at the warehouse.
  • Inspection & StorageCheck, photos and storage. Live labels sit in the app.
  • Shipping RequestedYou consolidate and book the international line.
  • Parcel PackedThe box is packed.
  • ShippedDeparture from China.
  • DeliveredDelivered; confirm receipt in the app.

The estimator is public; DIY Order is not

On 6 Oct 2026 the public estimator with destination a member state (form has no EU) (not a customs territory — pick ES/IE/IT), 1000 g and 35×25×10 cm showed among others pick ES/IE/IT in the form, n/a, n/a days, 0 lines. Amounts are USD on the official site and they change; open the estimator again. DIY Order on hipobuy.com opened the login wall. This desk does not fill in a DIY form.

Warehouse days are named in Help, not invented here

Official Help Center is a SPA (https://hipobuy.com/help). Desks document 90 free warehouse days from Stored; confirm that live Help copy the morning you ship (6 Oct 2026). This dest does not invent a storage window as a coupon. Orders stay on hipobuy.com. The 90-day line is on the official homepage under QC & Storage, not as a coupon-pack window.

First haul: flat first

T-shirts, shorts, jewelry for the first round. Down, bags, hats for the second. Electronics often mean lithium: check the line on the official site.
