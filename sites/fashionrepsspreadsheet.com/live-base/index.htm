Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying from China in 2026

What is FashionRepsSpreadsheet.com and how is it different from w2clinks.com?

FashionRepsSpreadsheet.com is this free strategy and guide website — it's where you come to learn how to shop from China, compare products, and find the best deals. w2clinks.com is the actual purchasing agent platform where you place real orders. Think of FashionRepsSpreadsheet.com as the map and w2clinks.com as the store.

What is a purchasing agent (daigou) and why do I need one?

Chinese platforms like Taobao and Weidian are mostly designed for domestic Chinese customers and don't ship internationally. A purchasing agent (daigou) like w2clinks.com acts as your middleman: they buy the item on your behalf, receive it at their Chinese warehouse, perform quality checks, and then ship it to your international address. They also handle any language barriers and payment issues.

How do I place an order on w2clinks.com?

It's straightforward. Find a product you want using our guides on this site, copy the Taobao/Weidian product link, and paste it into the order form on w2clinks.com. You'll see the estimated price in your currency, choose your size/variant, and pay. W2C Links handles everything from there. Don't forget to use coupon code SHIP for shipping savings.

How long does shipping from China take?

Shipping times vary by route and service level. Budget lines typically take 15–30 days; standard lines 10–18 days; and express lines 5–10 days. W2C Links offers multiple shipping options so you can choose based on your budget and timeline. All shipments include tracking numbers and are covered by insurance on request.

Are the products on the guides authentic?

Spreadsheet inclusion does not authenticate a product or guarantee quality. Review the live listing, seller evidence and quality-control photos, and avoid claims that cannot be independently verified.

Can I get help if I can't find what I'm looking for?

Absolutely. Join our community on Discord or Telegram. Drop a product image or description and our community will help you find the right link. You can also contact the w2clinks.com support team directly for agent-specific questions.
