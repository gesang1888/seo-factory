What is wemimi.net?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about browsing finds and using agent workflows.

What can I use wemimi.net for?

You can use wemimi.net to browse organized product categories, discover Weidian, Taobao and 1688 links, and prepare items for checkout through a third-party Chinese shopping agent.

Does wemimi.net sell products directly?

No. wemimi.net is a directory and guide site. It does not process orders, hold inventory, accept payments, or provide final shipping services. Purchases are handled through marketplaces and shopping agents.

How should I check an item before shipping?

After the item reaches your agent warehouse, review QC photos, measurements, size tags, color, logos, stitching, accessories and packaging. Ask for extra photos or request a return if something looks wrong.

Which agents can I use with these links?

Many users paste links into agents such as Superbuy, Sugargoo, AllChinaBuy, Mulebuy, Kakobuy, Hubbuy, PantherBuy and similar platforms. Always confirm that your chosen agent supports the item type, seller platform and destination route.
