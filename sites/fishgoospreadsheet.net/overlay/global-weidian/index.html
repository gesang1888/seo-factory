Global Weidian and FishGoo

FishGoo is on Weidian’s overseas purchase list. The list is not this website. This page tells you how to take a Weidian item into FishGoo and where the coupon actually lives.

Open FishGoo Coupon desk

Catalog, then list, then FishGoo

global.weidian.com browses products. The product click opens a Weidian item. Overseas buyers can see a Traceable list of agents on that item. FishGoo is one of those agents. Buy Now should open FishGoo for the same item. Copy Link is the URL people paste into chats.

If Buy Now only opens the FishGoo homepage, copy the Weidian URL that contains itemID and paste it into FishGoo search. FishGoo then parses title, price and options. Compare those fields with the Weidian page before you pay.

Keep the buy button off the shared list

This spreadsheet can use Global Weidian as a place to discover Weidian listings. The button that says buy should go to FishGoo, not back to weidian.com/item.html?offerId=. The offer-id URL opens the four-agent list again. QC photos of your own piece are taken after FishGoo receives it. Older photos in the finds index are other buyers’ references.

Traceable

The mark means Weidian is listing FishGoo as a purchase route for that item. It does not set the freight. Use the shipping guide to read line types, then the FishGoo estimator for the number you will pay.

FAQ

Did FishGoo show $1,000 or ¥3,500 on the list?

No. Those lines were on the USFans and OOPBUY rows. The FishGoo row had the Traceable mark and no coupon strip in the 18 November view.

Is FISHGOO10 a code for this?

No. This desk does not treat third-party typed codes as FishGoo coupons. Offers credit in the account coupon centre.

Is this website Weidian or FishGoo?

Neither. fishgoospreadsheet.net is an independent index. Pay on fishgoo.com.

Opening FishGoo from this page does not by itself attach a typed invite code. Coupons are issued on fishgoo.com.
