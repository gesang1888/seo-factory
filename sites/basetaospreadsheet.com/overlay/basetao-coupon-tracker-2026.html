What stacks, what does not, what voids

Stacking rules change with each event window. These are the patterns reported by buyers running real hauls in 2026.

Service-fee + shipping

Service-fee discount (BASE10) stacks with one shipping subsidy (BSHIP20). Dashboard preview should show both lines deducted before payment.

Two service-fee codes

Not stackable. Dashboard keeps the larger discount and silently drops the smaller — confirm the preview rather than assume.

Cart-value + shipping

BHAUL30 stacks with BSHIP20. Don't expect it to also combine with another cart-value code in the same checkout.

Account credit

BWELCOME5 and referral credits behave as wallet balance — applied after all coupon math against the final line. Always stackable.

Refund voiding

Returning a paid order may consume a single-use code without carrying it forward. Confirm with support before refunding a discounted order.
