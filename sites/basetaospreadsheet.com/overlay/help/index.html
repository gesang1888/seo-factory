Does BaseTao sell the goods on this site?

No. BaseTao is a purchasing agent: it buys from Chinese third-party shops in your name, photographs the parcel in its warehouse, then you book an international SKU. basetaospreadsheet.com is an independent information desk. Orders, payment and claims stay on https://www.basetao.com/. The cards on basetaospreadsheet.com are a reading of the finds index, not stock BaseTao owns. Payment never happens on this hostname.
