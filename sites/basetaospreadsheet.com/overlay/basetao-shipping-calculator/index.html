FAQ

Why is Basetao shipping expensive?

International freight, volumetric weight, and line choice all affect the final invoice.

Can I lower shipping before export?

Yes — remove boxes, split parcels, use rehearsal packaging, and pick a line matched to your country.

Where are real bill examples?

See our shipping bills and lines guides, then browse finds on the main spreadsheet.

Does this shipping calculator book the line?

No. It estimates. Pay and submit postage on basetao.com after QC. Sneakers and hoodies often bill volumetric weight, not the scale alone.
