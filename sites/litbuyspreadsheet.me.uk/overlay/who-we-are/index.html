How we work

Sources

Freight: public estimator at litbuy.com/shipping-estimate, dated. Duty: official sources for the destination, via ranked articles on this host. What we have not checked stays off the page.

Why so few live amounts in the prose

Lines move weekly. A number copied here would be wrong tomorrow. So: official estimator plus a dated screenshot, not an invented table.

Invite

The Register button may carry an affiliate code. Commission possible, no surcharge. You can join at litbuy.com without it. Uncomfortable facts stay published.

What we are not

Not a shop, warehouse, or ticket queue. Claims only on litbuy.com. We do not 301 this TLD onto another same-agent host or onto a hub.

Contact

If a lab column or a link breaks, we date it on News. We do not silently rewrite old checks. This desk does not answer order tickets. Editorial: support@litbuyspreadsheet.me.uk.

We do not invent company names or duty rates. Warehouse: 90 free days from stocked/listed, 120-day max (official Help Center, 1 Oct 2026). No declared-value coaching.
