Questions

Can I use a US ZIP?

No. Use a Canadian postal code — Canadian postal code A1A 1A1. Destination Canada in https://www.bbdbuy.com/estimation. A US ZIP is the wrong estimator country.

Where do I pay?

On www.bbdbuy.com. Last mile is often Canada Post. Display currency on this page is CAD.

When do I book freight?

After QC photos. Storage days are in Help on www.bbdbuy.com.

Who pays duty?

The booked SKU. Read https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/ (CBSA). This page does not invent a de-minimis dollar or a declared value.

Are English keys (sneakers, hoodie) a US shop?

No. The category wall is English because the index is English. Canadian words often return zero cards. Estimator country stays Canada. Those keys do not change CBSA or last-mile.

Two payments — is that an error?

No. Goods to China first, international freight after QC. Two receipts in the app are normal. If the app charges once, the app wins. www.bbdbuy.com.

Must I use this spreadsheet?

No. You can search the official app. The sheet saves dead Weidian cards toward a Canadian door. bbdbuy.ca does not see the account. Register only on www.bbdbuy.com.

Is a US mailbox the same SKU?

No. A US ZIP is another country in the estimator. Canadian postal code A1A 1A1 is the address test for this desk. Confirm destination Canada in https://www.bbdbuy.com/estimation the morning you ship.
