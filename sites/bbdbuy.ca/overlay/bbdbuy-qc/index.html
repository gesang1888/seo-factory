BBDBuy QC Photos before Shipping to Canada

QC on bbdbuy.ca means warehouse photos in the official BBDBuy app, taken in China, before you pay freight to Canada.

What QC is on this site

Quality check is extra angles of the piece in the warehouse — seams, tags, sole, print. It is not a laboratory, not a brand-authentication court, and not a CBSA x-ray.

Photos land in the official app while the SKU is still in China. That is the only window that matters for a retake.

When to ask for extra photos

Ask while the SKU is in the warehouse. After Canada Post has the carton, CBSA is the wrong office for a warehouse retake.

Extra angles are often a paid add-on in the official app. Open tickets in the BBDBuy app.

What QC does not do

QC does not file a CBSA entry, does not pay GST/HST on import, and does not invent a declared value. Educational source: https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/.

A clean QC album is not a promise the SKU will pass a later platform rule. Recheck before you book the line.

QC and a Canadian address

Use destination Canada and a Canadian postal code in the estimator. A US ZIP is the wrong country for this site. Canadian postal code A1A 1A1.

Warehouse photos are taken in China either way. The Canada part is the door on the label after you accept QC.

If a find fails QC

Leave it in the warehouse or return it in the official app. Do not book international freight on a reject just to 'get it over with'.

Failed QC is cheaper in China than after Canada Post attempts delivery.

Ready to ship after QC?

Only then open https://www.bbdbuy.com/estimation toward Canada. bbdbuy.ca does not take payment. CAD totals live in the app.

How to fill a Canadian delivery address

Use a Canadian street and postal code in the A1A 1A1 pattern. A US ZIP is the wrong country. Open https://www.bbdbuy.com/estimation with destination Canada.

Canadian postal code A1A 1A1 is the format the label needs.
