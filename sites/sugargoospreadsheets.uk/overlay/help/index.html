No. SugarGoo is a purchasing agent: it buys from Chinese third-party shops in your name, photographs the parcel in its warehouse, then you book an international SKU. sugargoospreadsheets.uk is an independent information desk. Orders, payment and claims stay on https://www.sugargoo.com/. The cards on sugargoospreadsheets.uk are a reading of the finds index, not stock SugarGoo owns. Payment never happens on this hostname.
