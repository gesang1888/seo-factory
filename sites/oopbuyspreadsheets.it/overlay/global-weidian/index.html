Where the click goes

global.weidian.com is a product grid. A product click opens the Weidian item with an offer id. Overseas buyers can then see Buy Now and Copy Link for each agent. OOPBUY is one row. USFans, RizzitGO and FishGoo were also on the list. Choosing a row is what assigns the order.

Link shape for a Weidian item

After you have the Weidian item id, OOPBUY’s product URL is:

https://oopbuy.com/product/weidian/{item id}?inviteCode=WEIDIAN

If you would rather not assemble it, paste the full weidian.com/item.html?itemID= link into OOPBUY search. OOPBUY reads the id. Use WEIDIAN on that visit so the Global Weidian banner and this page count as the same code.

Do not point a buy button at weidian.com/item.html?offerId=. That URL opens the shared list and offers the other agents again.

Traceable

The store mark labelled Traceable means the agent is presented inside Weidian’s overseas purchase list. It does not change Italian customs rules, warehouse storage, or the freight estimate on oopbuy.com/estimation. Photos of your own item still come from the OOPBUY warehouse after the seller delivers it there.

What to do with a find from this desk

  1. Pick the item on Global Weidian or on the spreadsheet.
  2. Copy the Weidian item link, not the short share link, if the short link hides the item id.
  3. Open OOPBUY with inviteCode=WEIDIAN and paste the link.
  4. Check size, price and domestic shipping, pay OOPBUY, then review QC before international shipping.
