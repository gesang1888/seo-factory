OrientDig QC Photos before Shipping to the UK

QC on orientdigspreadsheet.uk means warehouse photos in the official OrientDig app, taken in China, before you pay freight to the United Kingdom.

What QC is on this site

Quality check is extra angles of the piece in the warehouse — seams, tags, sole, print. It is not a laboratory, not a brand-authentication court, and not an HMRC x-ray.

Photos land in the official app while the SKU is still in China. That is the only window that matters for a retake.

When to ask for extra photos

Ask while the SKU is in the warehouse. After Royal Mail has the carton, HMRC is the wrong office for a warehouse retake.

Extra angles are often a paid add-on in the official app. Open tickets in the OrientDig app.

What QC does not do

QC does not file an HMRC entry, does not pay import VAT, and does not invent a declared value. Educational source: https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad.

A clean QC album is not a promise the SKU will pass a later platform rule. Recheck before you book the line.

QC and a UK address

Use destination United Kingdom and a UK postcode in the estimator. A French code postal is the wrong country for this site. UK postcode; Northern Ireland is often another carrier product.

Warehouse photos are taken in China either way. The UK part is the door on the label after you accept QC.

If a find fails QC

Leave it in the warehouse or return it in the official app. Do not book international freight on a reject just to 'get it over with'.

Failed QC is cheaper in China than after Royal Mail attempts delivery.

Ready to ship after QC?

Only then open https://orientdig.com/estimation/ toward the United Kingdom. orientdigspreadsheet.uk does not take payment. GBP totals live in the app.

How to fill a UK delivery address

Use a UK street and postcode on the label. Northern Ireland is often a different carrier product than mainland Royal Mail, so pick the live line in the app instead of swapping the country name.

Open https://orientdig.com/estimation/ and set destination United Kingdom before you pay international freight.

Prices in GBP

This page shows finds in GBP. You pay on orientdig.com — card, PayPal or balance, whatever the official account offers that day.

If the official screen still prints a dollar symbol, trust the number in the app and the calculator at https://orientdig.com/estimation/.

Paste a Taobao, 1688 or Weidian link

Copy the product URL from the Chinese shop, paste it on orientdig.com, and OrientDig buys it into the warehouse. orientdigspreadsheet.uk is the spreadsheet and guide for that path toward a UK door.

Sneakers, hoodies, jackets and bags are the usual rows because those SKUs still have QC angles. Electronics, perfume and liquids stay off the sheet unless the app allows them the morning you submit.

QC photos, then Royal Mail

Wait for warehouse photos in the app. Extra angles are often a paid add-on. After you accept QC, book the international line. Last mile is often Royal Mail.

Two payments are normal: goods into China, then freight. Storage days are listed in official Help on orientdig.com — read them the day you ship.

Import charges

Who pays VAT and handling depends on the booked SKU. Royal Mail can add a collect fee. Read https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad (HMRC) the morning you ship. This page does not invent a rate or a declared value.
