OrientDig Shipping to the UK (2026 Guide)

OrientDig shipping to the UK uses the official estimator country code GB, not this domain. Open the live freight form after warehouse photos. Last-mile is often Royal Mail.

How OrientDig shipping to the UK works

Warehouse in China → you accept QC → you book a line with destination United Kingdom → Royal Mail or the SKU’s last-mile partner hands it over. orientdigspreadsheet.uk does not print a live quote.

The label needs a real UK postcode. UK postcode; Northern Ireland is often another carrier product. Confirm destination GB in https://orientdig.com/estimation/ the morning you ship.

OrientDig shipping options for UK buyers

Tax-free, prepaid and collect are properties of the booked SKU in the official app. This page does not rename them. Collect can add a fee the estimator hides.

Royal Mail may also add a handling fee on collect parcels. Read the SKU, then the live estimator — not a screenshot from last year.

How long OrientDig shipping to the UK takes

Transit days change with the SKU and the line you book. This page does not invent Royal Mail business days.

Confirm in the official app the morning you ship. Warehouse processing is separate from international transit.

OrientDig shipping costs to the UK

You pay international freight plus whatever the SKU says at the border. Royal Mail can add collect handling. No pound amount is pasted into this title because the live form moves.

GBP is the local currency on this site. Checkout still happens on orientdig.com.

Customs, VAT and import fees

Read HMRC at https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad the morning you ship. Import VAT thresholds and Collect rules change. Ranked VAT articles on this site stay as local notes — they are not a live tariff table.

This page does not coach a declared value. Pay what the booked SKU and HMRC rules require.

Common shipping risks

Wrong country in the estimator (EU code postal), booking before QC, ignoring Northern Ireland as another carrier product. Those mistakes are UK-path errors, not 'agent is down'.

Restricted SKUs that slipped into the warehouse still cannot ride a UK line if the official app blocks them at submit.

Is OrientDig shipping suitable for first-time UK buyers?

Yes if you treat orientdigspreadsheet.uk as a map and orientdig.com as checkout. Address test: UK postcode. Source: HMRC.

Start with one clothing SKU, accept QC, then book. Do not consolidate a pile of un-QCd items on the first order.

Ready to ship safely to the UK?

QC first, then https://orientdig.com/estimation/ toward the United Kingdom. Orders only on orientdig.com.

How to fill a UK delivery address

Use a UK street and postcode on the label. Northern Ireland is often a different carrier product than mainland Royal Mail, so pick the live line in the app instead of swapping the country name.

Open https://orientdig.com/estimation/ and set destination United Kingdom before you pay international freight.

Paste a Taobao, 1688 or Weidian link

Copy the product URL from the Chinese shop, paste it on orientdig.com, and OrientDig buys it into the warehouse. orientdigspreadsheet.uk is the spreadsheet and guide for that path toward a UK door.

Sneakers, hoodies, jackets and bags are the usual rows because those SKUs still have QC angles. Electronics, perfume and liquids stay off the sheet unless the app allows them the morning you submit.
