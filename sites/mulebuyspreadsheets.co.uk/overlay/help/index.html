What MuleBuy is, and which language it uses

No. MuleBuy is a purchasing agent: it buys from Chinese third-party shops in your name, photographs the parcel in its warehouse, then you book an international SKU. mulebuyspreadsheets.co.uk is an independent information desk. Orders, payment and claims stay on https://mulebuy.com/. The cards on mulebuyspreadsheets.co.uk are a reading of the finds index, not stock MuleBuy owns. Payment never happens on this hostname. What you will pay

How many times do I pay?

Twice: first the goods plus China domestic shipping into the warehouse, later the international line to the United Kingdom. Between those payments you can consolidate. This desk never charges you; MuleBuy does, in-app. Warehouse photos sit between the two payments. Do not book international freight to the United Kingdom until you have accepted QC in-app.

On the official SPA the currency switcher has been observed to change the symbol without converting the digits. This desk converts the China warehouse card to GBP as a dated display (X-Rates 6 Oct 2026), not a checkout price. Re-read the number in-app the morning you pay. If the official SPA still prints USD digits after you pick GBP, trust the digits and https://mulebuy.com/estimation/, not this HTML. Display rate dated 6 Oct 2026.

Yes, warehouse QC photos land in the app after the piece arrives in China. Extra angles are often a paid add-on. What cannot ship?

Tobacco, alcohol, medicines and banned goods do not travel. Liquids, powders and pastes can need dangerous-goods paperwork. That is platform rule, not HMRC law. Restricted / price-0 cards on this catalogue are not buyable. Restricted is a platform purchase block, not a HMRC seizure notice. Price-0 cards on mulebuyspreadsheets.co.uk are not a shop error you can checkout.

Why is a card restricted or priced 0?

The source link is not available for agent purchase, or the price could not be read. Do not plan a haul around a zero. Transborder shops are often out of scope. Check the live MuleBuy product page. Price 0 often means the Weidian or Taobao link died. Open the live MuleBuy product page the same day; do not plan the haul on this HTML.
