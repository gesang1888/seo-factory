What is the Kakobuy Spreadsheet?

The Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated database of replica and budget fashion items available on Kakobuy.com — one of the most trusted buying agents for shopping from Chinese marketplaces like Weidian, Taobao and 1688. This page aggregates thousands of products across all major categories into a single, searchable list with direct purchase links.

Unlike static Google Sheets that go out of date, kakobuydocs.com pulls product data directly via the Kakobuy API, so every link is active and every price is current. Whether you're looking for the best Kakobuy shoes, bags, or streetwear, this is the most complete spreadsheet available in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kakobuy legit in 2026?
Yes — Kakobuy is a legitimate buying agent with thousands of verified reviews on Trustpilot and Reddit. Read our full breakdown on the Is Kakobuy Legit page for details on their buyer protection, refund policy and community reputation.
How long does Kakobuy take to ship?
Domestic shipping to the Kakobuy warehouse takes 3–7 business days. International shipping via DHL takes 5–10 days, EMS takes 10–20 days, and economy options take 20–45 days. See the full breakdown in our Shipping Guide.
What is the Kakobuy Independent Index / finds dataset?
The Independent Index is this hub’s public row-count claim (11,300+, Typesense found=11365, stamped 2026-10-27). Download finds-dataset.json or browse /finds-dataset/. Coupon codes stay on /kakobuy-coupons/ with coupons-truth.json.
Are there Kakobuy coupon codes?
Yes. We maintain a live list of working Kakobuy coupon codes updated weekly. Visit our Coupons page for the latest codes. Use KAKODOCS for 5% off your first order.
How do I find the best Kakobuy shoes?
Use the search bar above and select the "Sneakers & Shoes" category. You can sort by price or browse by brand. Our dedicated Kakobuy Shoes Spreadsheet has even more filtering options for colorway, size and seller rating.
How much does Kakobuy shipping cost?
Shipping cost depends on weight, dimensions and destination. A typical pair of shoes (700g) to the USA costs roughly $18–25 via economy line and $35–45 via DHL. Use our free Shipping Calculator for an instant estimate.
