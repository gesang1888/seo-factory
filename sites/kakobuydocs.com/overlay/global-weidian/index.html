Global Weidian and Kakobuy

Global Weidian’s purchase list, as seen on 18 November 2026, did not include Kakobuy. You can still buy the same Weidian item on Kakobuy by pasting the item link. Choosing Buy Now inside Weidian sends the order to an agent that is on the list.

Reviewed 18 November 2026.

The list that day was USFans, OOPBUY, RizzitGO, FishGoo, each with a Traceable mark. USFans and OOPBUY also showed coupon strips. Kakobuy has no row until Weidian adds it. This page does not pretend otherwise. Kakobuy’s own new-user offers stay on the coupon desk and on kakobuy.com.

Use Global Weidian as a catalog only

global.weidian.com is useful for finding a Weidian listing. Copy the item URL that contains itemID. A short share link from the Weidian app often hides that id; open it once in a browser and copy the final weidian.com/item.html address.

Paste that address into Kakobuy search. Kakobuy should show the title, picture, price and options. If the parse is wrong, stop and paste the link again. Pay on Kakobuy. Warehouse photos are taken after the seller ships to Kakobuy.

What the Weidian button does instead

Clicking the product on Global Weidian opens weidian.com/item.html with an offer id. The overseas purchase list on that page is the buy button. It offers the agents Weidian has placed there. Kakobuy is not one of them in the 18 November view, so that button cannot be the Kakobuy path. Spreadsheet links on this desk should paste through Kakobuy, not through the offer-id item URL.

When a Traceable row would matter

Traceable is the mark Weidian puts on agents it lists for overseas buyers. Until Kakobuy has that row, Global Weidian will not send a Buy Now click to Kakobuy. Pasting the link still works, because Kakobuy can purchase from Weidian without being in that list. A future row would be visible on the item page itself; this desk will only say Kakobuy is listed after that row is there.

FAQ

Can I use inviteCode WEIDIAN on Kakobuy?

No. WEIDIAN is the code on the OOPBUY banner of global.weidian.com. Kakobuy registrations stay on kakobuy.com. This page does not attach that code to Kakobuy.

Will a new domain named global kakobuy appear in the list?

No. The list is configured by Weidian. A domain does not add a row.

Where are QC photos?

After purchase, in the Kakobuy warehouse. The spreadsheet is an index of finds, not the photo of the unit you ordered.
