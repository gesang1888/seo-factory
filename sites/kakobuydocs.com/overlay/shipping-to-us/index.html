US fingerprint before you book a line

  1. Estimator destination: United States — pick US in the official app, not this hostname and not a Canadian postal code.
  2. Address — US street + ZIP. This hub does not file CBP entries.
  3. Duties — the booked SKU says tax-free / prepaid / collect. USPS or FedEx can add a collect fee the estimator hides. Source: CBP the morning you ship. No invented de-minimis dollar on this HTML.
  4. Last mile — often USPS or a commercial integrator after export handoff.
