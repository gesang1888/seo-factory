More ways to save on Kakobuy

Coupon codes are just one way to reduce costs. Here are additional strategies our community uses to save money on every order:

  • Consolidate shipments — order multiple items, store them free for up to 90 days, then ship together. One DHL parcel with 3 items beats three separate orders every time.
  • Use economy shipping lines — Economy Line A costs 55–65% less than DHL with only 1–2 weeks extra wait. For non-urgent orders this is the biggest saving available. See our shipping guide for the full comparison.
  • Rehearsal packaging — ask Kakobuy to repackage your items without their original boxes. This reduces volumetric weight and can cut shipping cost by 15–25%.
  • Time your orders around sales — Kakobuy runs promotions around Chinese holidays (11.11, Chinese New Year, Mid-year sale). Stock up during these windows for the biggest discounts.
  • Top up wallet during bonus events — Kakobuy occasionally offers wallet top-up bonuses (e.g. top up ¥500, get ¥50 free). Watch for these in your Kakobuy account notifications.
