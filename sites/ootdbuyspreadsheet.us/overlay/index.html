Questions

Does OOTDBuy sell the goods on this site?

No. OOTDBuy buys from Chinese shops in your name. ootdbuyspreadsheet.us is a spreadsheet toward a US ZIP. Checkout stays on ootdbuy.com.

Can I pay here?

No. Pay on ootdbuy.com. This page shows USD as display. Live freight is https://ootdbuy.com/estimation/ with destination United States.

Which estimator country?

United States — not Canada. A Canadian A1A 1A1 code is the wrong country. US ZIP.

Does QC happen at USPS?

No. Photos are taken in the China warehouse. USPS or FedEx is last-mile after you accept QC.

Who pays duty?

The booked SKU. This page does not invent a de-minimis dollar. Source: https://www.cbp.gov/ (CBP) the morning you ship.

Is a Canada Post SKU the same?

No. A Canadian postal code is a different country. Stay on this US desk for a US ZIP.

Why do local words return zero cards?

The finds index is English (sneakers, hoodie, jacket). That is not an empty shop.

Where is the invite code?

Not in this title. Existing coupon URLs on ootdbuyspreadsheet.us stay. New titles do not paste a code.
