What 0% fees actually mean

ACBuy, CNFans, Superbuy and Pandabuy often advertise 0% item fees. Revenue still has to come from somewhere — usually shipping markups. LitBuy states a service fee and may price freight closer to cost. Always compare item + fee + freight for your haul weight, not the headline percentage.

FAQ

Which agent is cheapest?

The cheapest checkout is the lowest landed cost after shipping, not the lowest advertised fee. Weigh a 2–5 kg haul on two agents before you standardise.

Which agent has the best QC?

Unlimited warehouse photos matter more than branding. ACBuy / AllChinaBuy, CNFans and LitBuy typically allow free QC on each item; some 0% agents cap free photos.

Fees and storage change. Verify on the agent's own site before you pay. This comparison is independent and not sponsored.
