How this AllChinaBuy calculator works

Shoppers searching all china buy customs, acbuy duty or allchinabuy import tax land here. Thresholds below are public de minimis / VAT starting points and change over time:

  • United States — de minimis treatment has been changing; treat sub-$800 figures as historical and verify CBP before you ship.
  • United Kingdom — VAT typically applies on imported goods; customs duty often starts above £135.
  • EU — VAT is usually due; customs duty commonly starts above €150.
  • Canada — general goods de minimis is low (often CAD $20); GST/HST may still apply.
  • Australia — GST often applies above AUD $1,000.
