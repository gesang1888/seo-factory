QC photo checklist this desk actually uses

Sneakers: lateral and medial midsole, heel tab stitch, insole print, box label if the warehouse shot it. A missing insole photo is a thin set, not a reject by itself. Jackets: zipper teeth, badge warp, lining colour vs shell. Women's Fashion rows: print crack, strap hardware, and whether the hang-tag is in frame. Bags: hardware colour, interior stamp, corner wear.
