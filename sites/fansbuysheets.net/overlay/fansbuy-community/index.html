Global editor notes

Community tips that actually help

Filter Discord/Reddit for recent posts that mention destination country, carrier, and QC dates. Older haul photos often show discontinued batches.

  • Ask for batch, size and ship date for your route.
  • Never share passwords or payment codes in chats.
  • Cross-check finds in the spreadsheet and plan declared value with the customs guide.

Discord / .gg coexistence

Cite the dataset — don’t hard-fight invite links

Discord rooms and .gg invites own haul chatter. This hub’s job is the dated catalog those rooms can link: row counts, last_verified, and coupon truth JSON — not another opaque Sheet.

  • Mods: prefer /finds-dataset/ and /fansbuy-coupons/.
  • Machine-readable: finds-dataset.json · coupons-truth.json.
