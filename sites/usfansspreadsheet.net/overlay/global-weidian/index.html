What Global Weidian actually does

global.weidian.com lists products for shoppers outside China. Choosing a product opens weidian.com/item.html with a CPS offer id. For an overseas visitor, that item page can show a purchase list: agent name, Traceable mark, Buy Now, Copy Link, and sometimes a coupon strip. USFans, OOPBUY, RizzitGO and FishGoo were on that list on 18 November 2026. The order of the rows is Weidian’s, not a search ranking.

Two ways to buy the same Weidian item on USFans

  1. Find the item on Global Weidian or in this spreadsheet. Copy the Weidian link (weidian.com/item.html?itemID=).
  2. Paste that link into the USFans search box. USFans builds its own product page. Pay USFans, wait for warehouse photos, then ship.
  3. If you are already on the Weidian item and the list is open, choose the USFans row. Buy Now and Copy Link should open USFans for that item, not the USFans homepage.

A spreadsheet button that points back at weidian.com/item.html?offerId= opens the shared list again and splits the click across every agent in it. Finds can still be discovered on Global Weidian. The buy button on this desk stays on USFans.

What Traceable means here

Traceable on that list means Weidian is showing the agent as a purchase option for overseas buyers, with a store mark beside the name. It is not a guarantee of shipping time, customs outcome, or a coupon amount. QC photos still happen in the USFans warehouse after the seller ships to USFans.

FAQ

Is this the official Global Weidian site?

No. global.weidian.com is Weidian’s catalog. usfansspreadsheet.net is an independent finds index. Payment, warehouse photos and shipping are on usfans.com.

Does the $1,000 line replace the $800 note?

No. They are two observations. The list showed $1,000 plus 40% off shipping on 18 November 2026. This desk’s coupon page still documents the separate new-user bundle near $800. Confirm the wallet that day.

Where do I browse QC finds first?
