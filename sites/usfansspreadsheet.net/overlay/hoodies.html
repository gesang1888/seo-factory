Tips before you buy

Tips for buying hoodies from USFans

  • Check the GSM (grams per square meter) on QC — heavier = more premium feel.
  • Hoodies are bulky; fold tightly or vacuum-pack at QC to save on shipping.
  • If sizing in inches isn't listed, ask the agent for chest/length measurements.
  • Premium prints can crack — request a flash photo of the print at QC.
