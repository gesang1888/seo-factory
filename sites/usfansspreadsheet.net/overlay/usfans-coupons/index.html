Checklist before you treat a coupon as “active”

  1. Open the coupon wallet after signup — screenshot each coupon’s min spend, max discount, eligible shipping lines, expiry.
  2. Estimate the haul on the live spreadsheet first; do not buy filler SKUs only to clear a minimum.
  3. Compare final freight after the coupon, not the headline percentage.
  4. Confirm whether the discount hits goods, service fees, or international shipping.
  5. Re-check terms the morning you pay — promotions rotate.

FAQ

Do I need a typed coupon code?

Usually not for the new-user bundle — coupons land in the wallet after referral signup. Always verify in-app.

Can I cash out the $800 figure?

No. It is a sum of constrained coupons, not withdrawable credit.
