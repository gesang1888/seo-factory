Check the coupon terms before signup

A headline bundle value is usually the sum of several coupons, not cash credit applied to one order. Before building a haul, open the coupon wallet and record each coupon's minimum spend, maximum discount, eligible shipping lines, expiry date and whether coupons can be combined.

  • Compare the final shipping total after the coupon, not the advertised percentage alone.
  • Do not add products only to reach a minimum spend; the extra item can erase the saving.
  • Check whether the discount applies to product payment, service fees or international shipping.
  • Take a screenshot of the terms before payment because promotions can change.
