Clothing QC checklist

Clothing shoppers often care less about one model name and more about fit, fabric, measurement and variant accuracy.

  • Compare chest, shoulder, length and sleeve measurements.
  • Check embroidery placement, print alignment and color accuracy.
  • Confirm variant names before submitting the order.
  • Group lighter clothing items to control parcel cost.

Clothing FAQ blocks

Question terms make the category page more useful than a plain product grid.

How do I pick a size from a USFans clothing spreadsheet?

Use the spreadsheet to find the item, then check seller measurements and warehouse QC photos. For clothing, measured garment dimensions are more useful than the size label alone.

Are hoodie and T-shirt variants important?

Yes. Many listings include color, batch, fabric and logo variations. Confirm the exact option before checkout so the warehouse purchase matches the item you wanted.

Should clothing be shipped together?

Usually it can be grouped, but total parcel weight and shipping-line restrictions still matter. Estimate before submitting the parcel.
