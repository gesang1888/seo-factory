Tips before you buy

Tips for buying tees from USFans

  • Cotton weight matters — 220gsm+ feels noticeably more premium than 180gsm.
  • For graphic tees, ask the agent for a flash QC photo to spot print issues.
  • Tees ship light — stack 4–5 to make your shipping cost-effective.
  • Pre-shrunk cotton is preferable; check the QC tag photo for wash instructions.
