Tips before you buy

Tips for buying shoes from USFans

  • Always check the sizing chart on the QC photo — Chinese sizing can run small.
  • Ask for an inner-sole shot if you're between sizes; toe-box width varies a lot.
  • Heavier shoes (boots, chunky sneakers) cost more in shipping — factor that in.
  • If you order multiple pairs, request 'remove shoeboxes' at QC to slash shipping volume.
