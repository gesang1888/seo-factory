For first-time visitors

What is a USFans Spreadsheet?

A USFans spreadsheet is a curated list of product links from Taobao, Weidian and 1688 that you paste into the USFans shopping agent. The agent buys the item, takes QC photos at the warehouse, then forwards your parcel.

This site is that list in website form. Use the live spreadsheet if you want to search; use the buying guide if you have not ordered through an agent before.

Frequently asked

USFans Spreadsheet FAQ

Where is the USFans spreadsheet?

The live sheet is usfansspreadsheet.net/usfans-spreadsheet/. That search page is the catalog. This homepage is the hub, not a second copy of the same landing.

Is this the official USFans website?

No. This is an independent fan-curated index. Orders, payments, warehouse QC and shipping happen on usfans.com. We are not affiliated with USFans, Taobao, Weidian or 1688.

Do I need to sign up to browse?

No. Search and category pages are public. You only need a USFans account when you are ready to place an order.

How long does USFans take to ship?

Domestic seller-to-warehouse is often 2–5 days. International shipping is commonly 10–20 days for budget air, faster for express, longer for sea. Confirm current lines at checkout.
