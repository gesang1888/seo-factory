USFans Haul Weight Estimator Guide

Know weight before QC ship — avoid bill shock.

Rough averages (per item)

  • Sneakers with box: ~1.2–1.8 kg
  • Hoodie: ~0.6–0.9 kg
  • Light jacket: ~0.8–1.2 kg
  • Winter puffer: ~1.5–2.5 kg
  • T-shirt: ~0.2–0.3 kg

USFans shows actual weight at warehouse — use QC stage to remove boxes and save grams. See shipping line comparison.
