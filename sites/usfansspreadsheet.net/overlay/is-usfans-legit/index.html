USFans trust and safety checklist

  1. Read recent reviews, not only the average. Filter for the newest critical reviews and look for repeated problems involving withdrawals, returns or missing parcels.
  2. Confirm the checkout total. Record exchange rates, payment fees, domestic freight, service fees and the international quote.
  3. Use QC as a decision point. Do not approve international shipping until the item, options and measurements match the order.
  4. Test support with a small order. Ask a specific return or shipping question and keep the response.
  5. Use a payment method with clear dispute terms. Never fund more than you can afford to have temporarily locked during a dispute.

Warning signs

Pause if the domain, payment recipient or support channel changes unexpectedly; if checkout fees differ from the published terms; or if you are pressured to approve QC quickly. Shopping-agent purchases also carry seller-quality, customs and international-return risk.
