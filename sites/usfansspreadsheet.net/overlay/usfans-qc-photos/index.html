USFans QC Photos: Inspection Checklist

Warehouse photos after the goods land in China — before you pay international shipping. A Google Sheet thumbnail is not this checklist.

QC (Quality Control) photos are warehouse pictures taken after your item arrives at USFans — before you pay international shipping. This spreadsheet surfaces 10,601 finds (last_verified 27 October 2026) that already have listing or community QC albums. The photos in the official app, for the SKU you paid for, remain authoritative.

What a Google Sheet cannot do

A shared Sheet usually pastes one thumbnail and a seller link. It does not date the photo, does not flag qc_photo in JSON, and does not tell you when to request a tape measurement versus when to return. This hub publishes:

  • A dated catalog stamp on /finds-dataset/ — row_count=10601, last_verified=2026-10-27.
  • Sample SKUs with an explicit QC flag in finds-dataset.json.
  • This page: category defects, approve / retake / return, packed-weight check before freight.

What to check in warehouse photos

  • Logo placement, stitching, and colour vs the seller listing you pasted
  • Size tag and insole / wash labels — ask for a tape if the tag is unreadable
  • Box, dust bag, and accessories if the listing promised them
  • Defects: glue marks, scuffs, stains, wrong variant, detached hardware
  • Packed silhouette: a puffy jacket can bill on volume even when the scale is light

Approve, request photos or return?

Approve

The item matches the ordered model, colour and size; measurements are within the tolerance you accepted; no visible construction or cosmetic defect affects use. Screenshot the set before you book freight.

Request another photo

Ask for a tape measurement when sizing is unclear, a close-up when stitching or print alignment is hidden, and natural-light colour photos when warehouse lighting changes the appearance. Extra angles are often a paid add-on in the app — confirm the live fee there, not on this page.

Return or exchange

Use the warehouse return window for the wrong item, wrong size, stains, damage, severe glue marks, detached parts or a material difference from the seller listing. Keep screenshots of the listing and QC set. After the parcel leaves China, that warehouse window is closed.

Final check: confirm packed weight and whether removing packaging changes volumetric shipping cost before submitting the parcel. Live money is in the official estimator.
