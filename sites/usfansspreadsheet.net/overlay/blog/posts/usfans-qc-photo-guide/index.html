USFans QC Photo Guide — What to Check Before Shipping

What to look for in USFans QC photos: defects, sizing, print quality, and when to request extra shots.

QC (Quality Check) photos are the most important step in the USFans buying process. Never approve shipment without reviewing them carefully.

What to inspect

  • Defects: loose threads, glue marks, scuffs, broken stitching
  • Color: compare against listing photos under natural light
  • Sizing: request chest/waist/length measurements — Asian sizing runs small
  • Prints: ask for flash photos on graphic tees to spot misalignment
