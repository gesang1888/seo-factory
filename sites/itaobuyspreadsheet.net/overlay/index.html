How an iTaoBuy order actually runs

iTaoBuy is a purchasing agent, not a shop. Official checkout is www.itaobuy.com. Freight estimate: www.itaobuy.com/freight-estimate. Help lives at www.itaobuy.com/help. iTaoBuy is not OotdBuy and not OssBuy — this desk only writes about iTaoBuy.
