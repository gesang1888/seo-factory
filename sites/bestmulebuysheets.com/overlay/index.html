What is the Mulebuy spreadsheet?

A Mulebuy spreadsheet is a searchable collection of product links from Chinese marketplaces that you purchase through the Mulebuy shopping agent. Each row typically includes the item name, price in CNY (converted to USD), source platform (Taobao, Weidian or 1688), category tag and a path to warehouse QC photos after purchase.

There is no single official spreadsheet published by Mulebuy. Instead, communities maintain curated lists — often as Google Sheets on Discord or Reddit. Those sheets go stale quickly: dead links, wrong prices and zero mobile filtering. BestMulebuySheets connects you to a live catalog of 8,000+ finds with category icons, individual SKU pages, an agent comparison tool and a step-by-step buying guide.

How Mulebuy fits in: Mulebuy is a China buying agent. You pick a product link, they purchase from the seller on your behalf, receive it at their warehouse in China, photograph it (QC), store it until you're ready, then ship internationally to your address. The spreadsheet is only the discovery layer — Mulebuy handles payment, inspection and logistics.

Taobao vs 1688 vs Weidian

All three platforms appear in the Mulebuy spreadsheet. Here's how they differ.

PlatformBest forPrice levelMin orderIn spreadsheet?
TaobaoConsumer goods, varietyRetail1 piece✓ Most common
1688Wholesale, bulk, factory directLowest per unitOften 2–5 pcs✓ Bulk deals
WeidianFashion, sneakers, streetwearVaries by seller1 piece✓ Streetwear heavy

Taobao is where most spreadsheet rows point — huge selection, single-piece orders, familiar to every China agent. Prices are retail-level but still far below Western streetwear markups for comparable items.

1688 is Alibaba's domestic wholesale site. Same factories as Taobao listings often sell here cheaper, but MOQs apply. Great for bulk hoodies or accessories if you're splitting a haul with friends.

Weidian is mobile-first and dominates sneaker and rep-fashion niches. Many sellers only list on Weidian — if a spreadsheet row says Weidian, don't try to find the same SKU on Taobao; paste the Weidian URL directly into Mulebuy.

F2

QC checklist before you approve shipping

Warehouse photos are your last chance to catch issues cheaply. International return shipping is rarely worth it.

Logo & brandingPlacement, font, spacing and embroidery vs listing photos and known retail references.
Stitching & seamsLoose threads, uneven hems, glue stains or misaligned panels — especially on sneakers and jackets.
Color & materialLighting in QC photos can lie. Compare to seller images and ask for extra angles if shade looks off.
Size tag & labelCheck printed size against what you ordered. Asian sizing runs small — verify measurements on the tag photo.
Accessories & packagingExtra laces, dust bags, boxes — note if missing when the listing promised them.
Weight & shapeBulky items: confirm dimensions look right. Agents weigh before international quotes — surprises cost money.

When in doubt, post QC photos to r/RepsQC or Discord QC channels before approving. A 24-hour pause beats a $80 return shipment.

Mulebuy spreadsheet FAQ

Quick answers — more on the full FAQ page.

What is a Mulebuy spreadsheet?+

A searchable database of Taobao, Weidian and 1688 product links you buy through the Mulebuy agent — with categories, prices and QC workflow built in.

How many products are in this spreadsheet?+

The live w2clinks catalog indexes 8,000+ verified finds across 33 categories. We also publish individual SKU pages with photos and buying steps.

Is this the largest Mulebuy spreadsheet?+

It connects to one of the largest maintained Mulebuy-compatible catalogs online — larger than static Reddit or Discord sheets, with search and category filters.

What are QC photos?+

Warehouse inspection photos taken after Mulebuy receives your item. Review stitching, color and flaws before paying international shipping.

Do I need a Mulebuy account?+

Browsing is free. To purchase, create a Mulebuy account — they handle seller communication, storage and worldwide shipping.

How often is the spreadsheet updated?+

The w2clinks catalog is maintained continuously. We run a weekly pass on our indexed pages and refresh category icons from the source.

How do I know if a spreadsheet link still works?+

Open the listing before ordering. If Taobao/Weidian shows "item removed," pick another row. w2clinks removes many dead links on their pass; we re-sync categories weekly.

What size should I order from Chinese listings?+

Always check the seller size chart — Asian sizing runs smaller than US/EU. Measure a similar garment you own and match cm measurements, not just "L" or "XL" labels.

Can I use a different agent than Mulebuy?+

Yes. Spreadsheet links work with most China agents — paste the same URL into ACBuy, CNFans, etc. We focus on Mulebuy because of QC workflow and community volume; compare on our agents page.

Will I pay customs or import tax?+

Depends on your country and declared value. Many EU/UK/US parcels incur VAT or duties above local thresholds. Choose accurate declaration on shipping — under-declaring risks seizure.

Is it safe to buy from Taobao and Weidian?+

Agents add a safety layer: they inspect at warehouse before you pay international shipping. Stick to spreadsheet-curated sellers with QC history; avoid random search results with zero reviews.
